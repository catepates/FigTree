Good to know

Do I need any experience?
None at all. Workshops are made for beginners.
When do I get my piece?
Cate takes everything away to be fired and glazed, then it's ready to collect a few weeks later.
Where are workshops held?
Venues change from session to session. Each listing shows where, and you register through the link on each workshop.
What should I wear?
Clothes you don't mind getting a little clay on.
What if I can't come?
Cancel 7 or more days before for a full refund of the ticket price or a transfer to another workshop. After that you can pass your place to a friend. If a workshop has to be cancelled, you'll get a full refund or a transfer to another workshop. See the workshop terms.
Can I book for a group?
Yes. Cate can run a private session for friends, a workplace or a celebration.

A birthday, a team day or a gift? Cate can bring a private workshop to your group.
