    About Cate

    Stories in clay

    Cate trained in ceramics in Cardiff, Wales (BA Hons) and has shown her work in New Zealand, Australia and the UK. She hand-builds every piece in earthenware, mixes her own glazes and draws directly onto the clay with ceramic pencils.
