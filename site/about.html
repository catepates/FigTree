About Cate

Cate Pates is a ceramic artist living in the western hills above Lower Hutt.

Born in England, she earned a BA (Hons) in Ceramics from the University of Wales Institute in Cardiff, Wales (UK). She makes unique pieces ranging from foxes, hares, owls and ravens to vessels decorated with her own drawings and poetry. Her work has been shown in solo exhibitions and galleries in New Zealand, Australia and the UK, and she runs clay workshops around Wellington.

“As a child I used to spend hours making animals out of fimo and telling stories with them. Now the materials may have changed slightly but the soul drive is still the same. Foxes, hares, owls and ravens hold spiritual significance for me.”

“I use earthenware clay and hand build each piece, using multiple firings to get the finish I desire. My glazes are made from scratch, and I draw directly onto the bisqued pieces with ceramic pencils.”

Cate Pates

“I like to include aspects of nature, and use my own poetry and drawing to invoke feeling and depth.”
