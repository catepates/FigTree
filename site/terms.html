Terms & privacy

These terms cover buying Cate's work from this website, commissions, and booking her clay workshops. They're written to be plain and fair. If anything's unclear, just ask.

Last updated 14 October 2026

Who you're dealing with

When you buy or book through this website you're dealing with a business, so you have the protections of the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and the Fair Trading Act 1986.

Pieces bought from ORA Gallery or The Vault are sold by those galleries, under their own terms.

Buying a piece

  • One of a kind. Every piece is hand-built, glazed and fired by Cate, so each is unique. Small variations in colour, glaze, texture and shape are part of the hand-making process and aren't faults. Photos are as accurate as possible, but screens vary.
  • Prices are in New Zealand dollars. No GST is charged.
  • Payment is taken securely by Stripe. Cate never sees your card details. A piece is yours once payment has gone through.
  • Care. Unless a listing says otherwise, pieces are decorative and not recommended for food, drink, the dishwasher, microwave or outdoors.

Delivery and pick-up

  • Cate sends pieces within New Zealand only, by tracked and insured courier, usually within 5 working days of your order. The delivery cost is shown before you pay.
  • Pieces are packed carefully. If yours arrives damaged, email or message Cate within 7 days of delivery with photos of the piece and the packaging, and keep the packaging. Cate will put it right: a repair where that's possible, otherwise a refund including delivery.
  • Pick-up is free and is arranged by email, at a public place.

Returns and refunds for artwork

This section covers pieces bought directly from Cate through this website, and commissions. Workshops and events have their own cancellation terms (see Workshops), and pieces bought from a gallery are covered by that gallery's terms.

  • Faulty, damaged or not as described: you're entitled to a repair, replacement or refund under the Consumer Guarantees Act. As every piece is unique, this will usually be a repair or a refund.
  • Change of mind: Cate doesn't offer refunds or exchanges if you simply change your mind, so please ask any questions before you buy.
  • To ask for a refund on a piece, use the contact form with your name, the piece and your order date. Cate will reply within 5 working days. Refunds go back to the card you paid with.

Commissions

  • Cate will agree the design, size, price and timing with you in writing before starting.
  • A 50% deposit starts the work. Because a commission is made just for you, the deposit can't be refunded once work has begun.
  • The balance is due when the piece is finished, before it's sent or collected. Handmade work can vary a little from sketches and samples.

Workshops and events

  • Booking is through Humanitix, which handles payment and sends your ticket. Humanitix's own booking fee isn't refundable unless Cate cancels.
  • If you can't come: cancel 7 or more days before the workshop for a full refund of the ticket price or a transfer to another workshop. With less than 7 days' notice the ticket isn't refundable or transferable, but you can give or sell your place to someone else.
  • In the unlikely event a workshop needs to be cancelled, you'll get the choice of a full refund, including booking fees, or a transfer to another workshop.
  • Absolute care and expertise is taken with firing and glazing each piece, but the nature of clay and kiln firing can be unpredictable, so Cate can't accept responsibility for cracks, breakages or glaze variations that happen during firing.
  • Cate sometimes takes photos at workshops for her website and social media. She'll ask first, and won't post you if you'd rather she didn't.

Privacy

Cate only collects what she needs to answer your message, send your order or run your workshop, under the Privacy Act 2020.

  • What's collected: your name, email and message when you use the contact form; your name, email, phone and delivery address when you buy (through Stripe); and your booking details and any answers you give at checkout when you book a workshop (through Humanitix). Cate won't sell or share your details, or add you to a mailing list without asking.
  • Where it's kept: with the services that run this site and its payments (Netlify, Stripe, Humanitix and Google), which may store it outside New Zealand.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see or correct the information Cate holds about you through the contact form.
  • This site doesn't use cookies. It counts visits with GoatCounter, which records which pages are viewed, the website or link a visitor came from, and their rough country and type of device, without identifying anyone.
